1 Introduction

The PNS can estimate the frequency of asthma diagnosis and describe some experiences of care among adults. These estimates depend on both the sampling design and the questions participants received. This report documents the indicators, their denominators and the limits of comparisons between 2013 and 2019.

The survey measures self-reported diagnosis at any time in life. It can reflect disease occurrence and access to diagnosis, but does not by itself identify active asthma, current treatment needs or annual service use (Menezes et al., 2015).

1.1 Methodological revision note

This version replaces the previous results after reprocessing the microdata. Corrections cover the age domain, eligibility pathways, cause classification and income handling. Earlier PNS results in the Word document were not used. Validation includes recoding, weights, estimators and agreement of bilingual outputs. The change history and execution files are available in the repository.

2 Data source and analytical scope

Official PNS 2013 and 2019 microdata are used. These are repeated cross-sectional surveys, with no individual follow-up in this analysis. The selected resident was aged ≥18 years in 2013 and ≥15 years in 2019. All results in this report use the common domain aged ≥18 years. No age or sex standardisation was performed.

Import uses labels = FALSE, retaining official codes. Source data are cached outside Git. The manifest records files, hashes, versions and design checks. Rendering reads previously computed results.

3 Implementation of the complex sampling design

The design is constructed for all selected residents before defining analytical domains. It uses UPA_PNS, V0024 and initial weight V0029, followed by post-stratification on V00293 using totals V00292. Each domain is explicitly paired with its total. Resulting weights are checked against V00291, with relative tolerance 10⁻⁶ for file precision; totals must be recovered with relative error below 10⁻¹⁰.

The object represents strata and clusters available for variance estimation; it does not explicitly reconstruct every selection stage or sampling fraction. Age, diagnosis and geographical restrictions are domain operations (Souza-Júnior et al., 2015; Lumley, 2010).

survey.lonely.psu = "adjust" handles strata with a single PSU. The additional option for lonely domains does not guarantee adjustment in post-stratified designs. Outputs include PSU and stratum counts, degrees of freedom, strata with only one PSU in the domain (lonely_strata_n) and estimability status. This count flags a precision limitation; a calculable CI does not remove that limitation.

4 Estimators and confidence intervals

Proportions and means are ratios of weighted sums among eligible respondents with valid information. Ignored responses, unknown eligibility and inapplicable questions are distinguished during recoding. The missing column in the tables is the weighted percentage of invalid information within the eligible domain. Missing answers to preceding questions can prevent eligibility from being determined.

Proportions use svymean() for the estimate and standard error and svyciprop(method = "beta") for the 95% CI (Korn and Graubard, 1998). Means use a t approximation with domain degrees of freedom. Observed proportions of zero or one are reported with a non-estimable CI; empty domains and unavailable variances have explicit statuses.

Differences in diagnosis, attacks and smoking are expressed in percentage points. The standard error combines edition-specific variances under an independence approximation; the CI uses Welch–Satterthwaite degrees of freedom. Covariance between editions is not estimated. National contrasts are prioritised; state contrasts are exploratory, without confirmatory interpretation. Overlap of individual CIs does not test difference or equivalence.

9.5 Composite individual monthly income

The sum of E01602, E01604, E01802, E01804, F001021, F007021, F008021 and VDF00102 is retained. It includes cash and estimated in-kind earnings from the main and other jobs, retirement/pension income, alimony/donations/allowances, rent and the aggregate of other income sources. There is no filter based on formal employment status.

Zero is assigned only when the questionnaire establishes non-receipt of the source. Receipt without a valid amount or unknown eligibility leaves the component unknown and prevents calculation of the total. Known totals of zero are included. Special codes are variable-specific; the dictionary does not identify 99999999 as ignored in VDF00102.

The official deflator expresses nominal amounts at July 2019 prices; the national IPCA then updates them to August 2025, without intermediate rounding. The measure is subject to coverage limitations and underreporting. It is not household per capita income, disposable income or a direct measure of informality. It will not be multiplied by restriction days to monetise productivity.

10 Interoperability and reproducibility

Tables have stable identifiers and fields for eligibility, precision and provenance. Future integration with healthcare records must preserve the cross-sectional nature and design of the PNS. The survey does not provide individual care trajectories. OMOP/OHDSI documentation principles can inform complementary sources while respecting these differences (OHDSI, n.d.).

11 Interpretation and limitations

Comparisons of diagnosis, attacks and smoking depend on equivalent definitions and the approximation used for variance across editions. Changes in composition and access to diagnosis are possible explanations, without causal identification. Changes are not attributed to the number of respiratory physicians. Medication results require edition-specific interpretation.

Payment does not quantify costs, and respiratory causes do not identify asthma exclusively. Economic analysis for 2023–2025 requires additional sources, attribution rules and consideration of the time gap.

Excluding unknown information can introduce bias if nonresponse is associated with the outcome. Composite income requires sufficient information for all eight components, potentially selecting people with more complete records. Outputs record missingness and precision to support this assessment.

12 Validation and reproduction files

tools/run_analysis.R runs the complete analysis. Indicator entry points call the same implementation. Results pass through a staging area before publication. The manifest checks agreement between code, tables and report. Tests cover recoding, income, design, estimators and bilingual outputs. Commands and results are recorded in the validation log.
